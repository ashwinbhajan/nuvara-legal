AI disclosure

Nuvara uses third-party AI services to write and voice your stories. We only send your answers to them after you tap "I agree" on the consent screen. You can withdraw at any time in My world, and Nuvara will switch to browsing the library only.

ServiceRole
AI text serviceWrites your personal story from your answers
AI voice serviceTurns the story into spoken audio, and writes the story if the first service is unavailable
  • Your data is not used to train AI models.
  • Stories are checked by our own rules before you hear them, including a list of things you asked us never to mention.
  • The voices you hear are AI-generated, not recordings of real people.
  • Writing stops and a support screen is shown if your words suggest you may be in crisis.
  • You can report any story from the player.

Nuvara is for general wellbeing and reflection. It is not a substitute for professional medical, mental health or financial help. For adults aged 18 and over.
