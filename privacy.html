Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026. This policy is a working draft and should be reviewed by a privacy professional before launch.

Who we are

Nuvara ("we") is operated from the European Union. Contact: support@nuvara.app.

What we collect

  • Your answers to the onboarding questions: a name you choose, areas of life, short free-text answers, feelings, interests, things you never want mentioned, your chosen voice and listening time.
  • People you add: first name and relationship only (for example "Sam, friend"). We store nothing else about them and delete it with your account.
  • Your stories: the written script, the audio, and feedback you give on a story.
  • Account and purchase data: an anonymous account id, or the Google or Apple identity you choose to link, and your subscription status.
  • Technical data: a device identifier used to limit one free story per device, crash reports, and basic usage events.

We do not ask special-category questions (health conditions, sexuality, religion, politics, ethnicity). Free-text answers can still contain personal details, so please do not share sensitive information there. We treat all answers as confidential.

We use paid, business tiers of AI services whose terms exclude training on our data. We do not use advertising SDKs and we never sell or share your data for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Audio of unused free stories: deleted after 90 days.
  • Inactive anonymous accounts: deleted after 12 months.
  • When you delete your account: your data is removed, and audio and files are deleted within 24 hours by design, and in any case within 30 days.

Your rights

You can export your data and delete your account inside the app (Settings). You can also ask to access, correct, restrict or object to processing, or complain to your data protection authority. Email support@nuvara.app.

Children

Nuvara is for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app and, where needed, ask for consent again.

Nuvara is for general wellbeing and reflection. It is not a substitute for professional medical, mental health or financial help. For adults aged 18 and over.
